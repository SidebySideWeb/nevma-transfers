Dropdowns

One dropdown everywhere: a button with role="combobox" and a role="listbox" list on the raised surface with a gold top edge, a gold check on the selected option and optional right-aligned meta (free count, price). Arrow keys, Home/End, type-ahead, Enter/Space pick, Escape and Tab close, click outside closes. The native <select> stays in the form for posting. Disabled options stay visible with the reason ("0 free").
